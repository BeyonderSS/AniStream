# Disclaimer

AniStream is an unofficial client. It does not host, upload or store any video; everything it plays is fetched on demand from third-party, non-affiliated websites, exactly as a web browser would.

The project exists to explore building a clean, native Android experience on top of publicly reachable content. Whether streaming that content is legal depends on where you live: **use it at your own risk and in line with your local laws.** If you are a rights holder and something you own is reachable through a site this app talks to, contact that site — AniStream has no control over it.

The scraping approach is ported from [ani-cli](https://github.com/pystardust/ani-cli) (GPL-3.0), which this project is licensed under as well.
